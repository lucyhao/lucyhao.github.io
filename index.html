About

I'm Hao Li. I spent over a decade as a front end developer across China and Singapore — Qihoo 360, Tencent, Garena, and a mix of other companies big and small along the way.

In 2024 we moved to the UK. Back in China I had grandparents to help with childcare; in Singapore, a live-in helper. In the UK, it was just me — and I found out I was pregnant with my second not long after we landed. So I did the obvious thing and became a full-time mom. Turns out it's harder than any job I've ever had. 😂

Now that my younger one has just turned one, I've finally clawed back a sliver of time to myself — so I'm back to writing code(vibe coding 😂), one hand on the keyboard and the other on a toddler. And if this whole "working" thing doesn't pan out, I've got a backup plan: move back to my hometown in Sichuan and sell fried potatoes on the street.

What I've built

AllyBaby Allergy tracker for breastfeeding families

Tracks both mom's diet and baby's symptoms to automatically surface which foods in breast milk may be triggering a baby's reactions — the only allergy tracker built specifically for nursing families.

When my daughter was three months old, she developed eczema — her face and knees turning dry, red, and itchy, until it got bad enough to start weeping. I took her to the GP more times than I can count, but nothing really helped. Since I was exclusively breastfeeding, I suspected something in my own diet was the trigger — but with dozens of things to eat every day, I could never remember what I'd actually had. That's how AllyBaby was born: a way to log what mom eats and what baby eats, side by side, to help find the pattern. I like to think of it as a gift for my daughter.

GoOutToday Today's day out, decided for you

Picks a family day out around today's weather, your drive-time limit, and your kids' ages — free on iOS, currently covering Greater Manchester & the North West of England.

We also have an older son, and weekends always left us stuck on the same question: where do we take him today? I'd scroll through Facebook groups and Xiaohongshu for ideas, but there was either too much to sift through, or I'd be hunting one post at a time. So I built an app that just recommends a place directly — and lets me set a reminder so I don't forget it. Just last week, with no idea where to go, it suggested somewhere we'd never been. The whole family went, and came back thinking, "actually, that was pretty good." I like to think of it as a gift for my son.
